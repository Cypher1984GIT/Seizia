Help & Features

1 Unified AI Tabs

Keep multiple AI chats open in a single window. Switch between ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and more instantly without losing context. While a new tab loads, Seizia shows a loading screen so you don't see a flash from the site. Drag tabs to reorder them. Close with the X; reload appears on hover.

2 Split View

Compare responses side-by-side. Needs at least two open AI tabs. If you have more than two, choose which pair to split.

Ask All AIs

Type a prompt once and send it to all open AI tabs. Use Save in that window to store it in My prompts.

Prompt library

Open the book icon in the header. Library has ready-made prompts. My prompts is yours — add one with Add prompt, or save a copy from the catalog. Categories lets you add, rename, or delete any category, including the defaults. Deleted categories send their prompts to Uncategorized, which cannot be renamed or deleted. Use sends it to the active AI (you can edit first), or to all AIs if you have two or more tabs. Your prompts stay on this computer even if you reset tabs.

3 Incognito Mode

Open a tab in Incognito to skip saving that session. Marked with glasses on the tab. AIs that require a login (Claude, Poe, DeepSeek, etc.) cannot be opened in Incognito.

4 Custom AIs

Don't see your favorite tool? Add any website or local AI as a custom tab.

⋯ More menu

The ⋯ button opens Help, License, and Reset. Reset clears open tabs and returns Seizia to defaults; it does not delete My prompts or your license.

Light & dark theme

Use the sun/moon button in the header. The theme applies to Seizia and is passed to open AI tabs when the site supports it.
