What it does, without the pitch

It is not a new model. It is the window that holds the ones you already use. The chat still lives on chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, and the rest. Seizia does not keep your conversations on a server.

  1. 01 Tabs Several AIs open at once. Drag to reorder. Sessions survive a restart.
  2. 02 Ask All Type once. Every open tab gets the prompt.
  3. 03 Split Two AIs side by side. If more than two are open, you pick the pair.
  4. 04 Prompts A ready catalog plus your own, stored on disk. Categories included.
  5. 05 Incognito A tab that leaves no session. AIs that require a login stay out.
